4 Golden Rules for Every Patient

Simple guidelines to keep you and your loved ones safe.

⏱️
Finish Antibiotic Courses

Stopping early can leave resistant bacteria alive, making future infections much harder to treat.

🍱
Stomach Protection

Pain medicines like Ibuprofen can irritate your stomach lining. Always take them with food or milk.

🔍
Check Hidden Paracetamol

Many cold and flu syrups contain paracetamol. Be careful not to take double the safe daily amount.

🩺
When in Doubt, Ask

Pharmacists and doctors are there to help. Never hesitate to double-check dosage or interactions.
